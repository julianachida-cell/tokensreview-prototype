Medical Plan — In-Network Benefits

Your Blue Shield Silver PPO 2026 plan covers preventive care at no cost and shares costs after you meet your deductible.

Copays & Coinsurance

Once you meet your deductible, you'll pay a copay of $25 for each primary care visit, and $50 for visits with a specialist. After that, the plan pays 80% of covered services and you pay the remaining 20% until you reach your out-of-pocket maximum.

Premiums

For the 2026 plan year, your monthly premium contribution for individual coverage is $250. Family coverage premiums vary by tier — see the enrollment portal for personalized rates.

Prescription Drug Coverage

Generic medications have a copay of $10, brand-name drugs are $40, and specialty medications are subject to 25% coinsurance after deductible.
